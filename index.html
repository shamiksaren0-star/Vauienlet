About Me

My background, interests, and approach to independent game development.

I am an independent game developer and the founder of Vauienlet Studios, an independent game development brand focused on creating interactive software and games.

My technical interests include C programming, C# programming, Unity game development, gameplay mechanics, player controls, projectile-based combat, and enemy behaviour.

I have developed independent game projects and prototypes while learning to implement gameplay systems, troubleshoot code, and improve my development workflow.

I am currently pursuing a Diploma in Game Development at Red Apple Learning. I am continuing to strengthen my programming fundamentals and practical development skills.

My long-term goal is to become a hybrid developer who can contribute to programming and collaborate across creative and technical disciplines in the game and software industries.
